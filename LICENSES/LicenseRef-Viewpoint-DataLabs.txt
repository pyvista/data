Viewpoint Animation Engineering / Viewpoint DataLabs dataset terms

Source: the header of the dataset files themselves.

The Viewpoint models redistributed in VTK's test data carry their terms in
their own headers, quoted verbatim from `cow.obj`:

    (c) Copyright 1992 Viewpoint Animation Engineering Inc.
    Sun Microsystems has been authorized to freely distribute
    these Datasets.  They are provided for unlimited use in
    existing or modified form.  The actual Dataset (ie, geometry)
    may not, however, be resold in existing or modified form.

This is not an OSI-approved or Creative Commons license. It permits
redistribution and modification without restriction, but the geometry itself
may not be resold in any form. A product that sells the model, or a dataset
derived from it, is outside the grant; a product that merely uses it is not
addressed either way.

The deleted `Data/Viewpoint/README` recorded that these datasets came from
Viewpoint DataLabs International, which sold a commercial model library.
