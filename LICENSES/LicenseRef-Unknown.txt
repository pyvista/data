Undetermined License

This identifier marks a dataset whose licensing could not be established.

It is used when the origin of a file could not be traced to a source that
states terms of use — typically a file inherited from an upstream test-data
collection that carries no per-file provenance, or a mesh whose only
recorded history is a redistribution by a third party.

A dataset carrying this identifier:

  - has a `notes` field describing what was and was not established, and
  - MUST NOT be assumed to be freely licensed.

It says nothing about `provenance`, which records confidence in the origin
rather than in the licence. A dataset can have a certain source and
undetermined terms: the Laser Design scans are `provenance = "verified"`
because the archive they came from is known, and carry this identifier
because that archive grants no rights.

Downstream users redistributing these files, especially in a commercial
product, should establish the terms independently or substitute a dataset
with documented licensing. Anyone who can identify the origin of one of
these files is asked to open an issue at
https://github.com/pyvista/data/issues.
