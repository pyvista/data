Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory — 3D Scanning Repository
Conditions of Use

Source: http://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/

The Stanford 3D Scanning Repository publishes the following conditions of
use, quoted verbatim from the repository page:

    "You are welcome to use the data and models for research purposes.
    You are also welcome to mirror or redistribute them for free.
    Such models or images are not to be used for commercial purposes,
    nor should they appear in a product for sale (with the exception of
    scholarly journals or books), without permission from the Stanford
    Computer Graphics Laboratory."

The repository additionally carves the bunny and the armadillo out of its
cultural-sensitivity restrictions:

    "You can do anything you want to the Stanford bunny or the armadillo."

Required acknowledgement: credit the Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory.

This is not an OSI-approved or Creative Commons license. It permits free
redistribution and research use but restricts commercial use, so datasets
under these terms are not safe to embed in a product for sale without
first contacting the Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory.
