Utah Teapot — Public Domain By Convention

The Utah Teapot was modeled by Martin Newell at the University of Utah in
1975. No formal license has ever been issued for the original dataset.

The model has been redistributed freely in essentially every computer
graphics package since the mid-1970s — POV-Ray, Blender, 3ds Max, the
OpenGL Utility Toolkit and VTK among them — and is universally treated as
public domain by the computer graphics community. The Computer History
Museum holds the original teapot; the dataset itself has never been
asserted as a proprietary work.

Recommended attribution:

    "Utah Teapot, originally modeled by Martin Newell,
    University of Utah, 1975."

This identifier records a community convention rather than a grant of
rights from an identified copyright holder. Downstream users who need a
formal grant should treat the status of this dataset as undetermined.
