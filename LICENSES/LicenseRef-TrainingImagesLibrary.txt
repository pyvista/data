Training Images Library terms

Source: http://www.trainingimages.org/training-images-library.html

The training images library accompanies the book "Multiple-point
Geostatistics: Stochastic Modeling with Training Images" by Gregoire
Mariethoz and Jef Caers. Its download page introduces the images with:

    Below are some of the training images that are employed in the book.
    They are presented both graphically and in ASCII format. These images
    be used freely for research purposes. Enjoy!

That grant is limited to research. Nothing on the page permits commercial
use, and no other terms are published, so commercial use is treated as not
granted. Redistribution for research purposes, which is what this repository
does, is within the grant.
