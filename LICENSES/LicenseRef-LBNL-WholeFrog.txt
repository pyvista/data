Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Whole Frog Project data terms

Source: the `Copyright.Notice` file published beside the data at
https://froggy.lbl.gov/images/whole.frog/data/ (Whole Frog Project,
Information and Computing Sciences Division, Lawrence Berkeley National
Laboratory, 1993-1995). This is not an SPDX-listed licence; the wording it
is based on is quoted here.

The notice states:

    "These images and data were produced at Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory
    by Bill Johnston and Wing Nip of the Information and Computing
    Sciences Division ... This data is provided for the purpose of
    scientific research and collaboration with Lawrence Berkeley
    Laboratory. Any commercial use of these images and data require prior
    agreement with Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory. The images and data are
    Copyright Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory."

What this means for a downstream user:

  - the data may be used for scientific research;
  - the copyright and the credit to Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory must be kept;
  - any commercial use needs a prior agreement with the laboratory.
