Utah Teapot terms of use

Source: https://graphics.cs.utah.edu/teapot/, the University of Utah
Graphics Lab's Utah Teapot page ("the official Utah Teapot page"), read on
2026-09-28; the page's curly quotation marks are rendered straight here.
Background: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_teapot. This is not an
SPDX-listed licence; the wording it is based on is quoted here.

Martin Newell created the dataset at the University of Utah in 1975. No
formal licence was ever issued for the original data, and for decades the
model circulated on that basis: Wikipedia records that versions of it "are
distributed with or freely available for nearly every current rendering and
modeling program and even many graphic APIs". The University of Utah's
page now states terms, under the heading "Terms of Use and Attribution",
verbatim:

    "The Utah Teapot model files are freely available for any use,
    including commercial use. To preserve the historical identity and
    provenance of the model, users are requested to identify the model,
    and any substantially derived version of it, as a Utah Teapot (or
    Modified Utah Teapot) and to acknowledge its origin as the historic
    Utah Teapot developed at the University of Utah. This request is
    intended solely to preserve the historical identity of the Utah Teapot
    and is not intended to restrict use of the model. The model files are
    provided "as is," without warranty of any kind. The provider of these
    files shall not be liable for any claims, damages, or other liability
    arising from their use."

What this means for a downstream user: any use, including commercial use,
is permitted. Credit is requested, not required, which is why
`attribution_required = false`. The page asks that the model be identified
as a Utah Teapot (or Modified Utah Teapot) and its origin acknowledged as
the University of Utah; the `attribution` field of each entry here is this
repository's wording of that request, not a form the page prescribes.

The page does not use the words "public domain", and no rights statement
by Martin Newell was found, so this identifier is a stated permission plus
a convention, not a public-domain dedication. The copies here (`teapot.g`,
`TeapotPoints.txt`, `teapot.wrl`) came through VTK and the VTK Examples,
not from that page; their entries record the route.
