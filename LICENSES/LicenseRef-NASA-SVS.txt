NASA Scientific Visualization Studio Media Usage

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/

NASA content — images, video and audio produced by the Scientific
Visualization Studio — is generally not copyrighted and may be used for
educational or informational purposes, including photo collections,
textbooks, public exhibits, computer graphical simulations and internet
web pages, without prior permission.

NASA's media usage guidelines require that:

  - NASA content may not be used to imply NASA endorsement of a product
    or service.
  - Content that includes identifiable people may not be used to imply
    their endorsement.
  - Third-party material incorporated into a NASA product remains subject
    to the rights of its owner.

Datasets in this repository derived from NASA SVS releases carry the
acknowledgement required by the specific release, recorded in the
`attribution` field of the dataset's entry in DATASETS.toml.

Full guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
