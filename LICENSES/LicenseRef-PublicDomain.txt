Public Domain

Used for work that is in the public domain, where the reason is recorded in
the `notes` of each dataset that carries this identifier. The reason matters,
because it decides who else the status binds:

  - Copyright has expired. The work is old enough that no term still runs
    anywhere the repository is used.

  - It is a work of the United States federal government. 17 U.S.C. Sec. 105
    denies copyright to works prepared by federal employees in the course of
    their duties. This does not extend to contractor-produced work, nor to
    the same work in other jurisdictions.

  - The author dedicated it to the public domain in their own words, rather
    than through CC0 or another instrument. Such a dedication is recorded
    verbatim in the dataset's `notes` so a reader can judge it.

No permission is needed, no credit is required, and there is no restriction
on commercial use. Crediting the author is still good practice, and each
entry records who that is where it is known.

Do not reach for this identifier because a source is silent about its terms.
Silence is `LicenseRef-Unknown`. This one is for a status that has been
established and can be pointed at.
